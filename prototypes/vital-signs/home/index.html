Puget Sound Vital Signs

Vital Signs en Español

The Puget Sound Vital Signs are measures of ecosystem health and progress toward Puget Sound recovery goals. Thanks to a strong network of monitoring programs and committed people in the Puget Sound Ecosystem Monitoring Program , the status and trends of indicators are regularly reported throughout this website.
